Privacy Policy — Clipper for Rumble

Last updated: 2026-11-20

The short version: this extension collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no servers. Clips are captured, edited, rendered, and stored entirely on your own device, in your browser.

What the extension accesses

  • The video you are watching on rumble.com. When you use a clip action, the extension captures the playing video and its audio using your browser's built-in media APIs, on your device.
  • An optional replay buffer. If "Instant-replay buffer" is enabled in settings, a rolling buffer of recent playback is kept in memory only while a Rumble tab plays, so "Clip last 30s" works instantly. Buffered media is discarded continuously and is only saved if you explicitly clip it. You can turn the buffer off at any time.
  • Local storage. Clips you save, exports you render, and your settings are stored in your browser's local extension storage (IndexedDB / chrome.storage) on your device. Settings may sync between your own signed-in Chrome browsers via chrome.storage.sync; they are not sent to us (we have no server to receive them).

What is sent over the network

Nothing. The extension makes no network requests of its own. It contains no analytics SDKs, no remotely hosted code, and no third-party services. When you click a "Post to…" button, your browser simply opens that platform's normal upload page in a new tab; anything you then upload there is governed by that platform's own privacy policy.

Data sharing

We do not collect, sell, rent, or share any personal data, browsing history, or media, because none of it ever reaches us.

Deleting your data

Use the trash controls in the clip editor, or "Delete all clips & exports" in Settings, or uninstall the extension — uninstalling removes all locally stored clips, exports, and settings.

Children

The extension is a general-purpose media tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If a future version ever changes this policy, the updated policy will be published at this URL and the version above will change before the new behaviour ships.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on the project repository: https://github.com/VetTV-Studios/rumble-clipper/issues.

Clipper for Rumble is an unofficial tool and is not affiliated with Rumble Inc.
